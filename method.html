We are an identity laboratory that translates your core values into unique immersive architectural experiences.

More than the sum of function, form, and materials, we design the deliberate sequence of perceptions, emotions, and interactions that allows people to experience your identity.

  1. 01

    Client identity

    We begin by listening. Who you are, and what you mean to stand for. What are the values that make your foundation, what is the story that is uniquely yours: that’s where we start.

  2. 02

    Concept

    From that we craft the narrative we want to express on a specific site. Architectural elements are the characters of this book. Requirements, constraints, program, code — they do not write the story. They are the page the story is written on.

  3. 03

    Technical design

    Most offices begin here. We arrive with the narrative already written, and detail it through schematic design, design development, construction documents, and construction administration. Licensed in New York. We lead the consultant team.

  4. 04

    Occupancy

    The space is yours. It is an extension of your identity. It says what you came to say. Visitors will tell.
