We are an identity laboratory that translates your core values into unique immersive experiences.

Identity is not a logo.

Identity is not a website.

Identity is not a marketing campaign.

Identity is what remains when everything superficial is stripped away.

It is your purpose, your convictions, the reason your organization exists, and the lasting impression you leave on everyone who experiences your space.

Most architects treat identity as a veneer, while branding treats space as background. We believe the opposite.

Architecture is truly powerful when it becomes the physical embodiment of your identity. It is the strongest medium for expressing identity.

More than the sum of function, form, and materials, we design the deliberate sequence of perceptions, emotions, and interactions that allows people to experience your brand.

  1. 01

    Client identity

    We begin by listening. Who you are, and what you mean to stand for, is settled before a line is drawn.

  2. 02

    The story

    The story to be told, and the values it has to carry. We write it, and we rewrite it, until the identity has a language. Form comes after that language, not before.

  3. 03

    Concept

    We survey the site as it is: where it is, what it is, what it must do. Requirements, constraints, program, code, read in full. They do not write the story. The site is the page. The concept is how that page is shaped to carry it.

  4. 04

    Technical design

    Most offices begin here. We arrive with the narrative already written, and hold it through schematic design, design development, construction documents, and construction administration. Licensed in New York. We lead the consultant team.

  5. 05

    Occupancy

    The space is yours. It says what you came to say.
